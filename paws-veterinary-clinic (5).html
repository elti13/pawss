FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Still have a question? Call us at (404) 555-0187 or send a note through the form below.

How do I make an appointment?+
You can request an appointment online using our scheduling form or call our clinic during business hours at (404) 555-0187.
Do you accept same-day appointments?+
We reserve a limited number of appointments for urgent concerns each day. Availability varies, so we recommend calling as soon as possible.
Do you treat emergencies?+
We provide urgent veterinary care during regular business hours. For life-threatening emergencies outside our hours, we'll direct you to a nearby 24-hour emergency veterinary hospital.
What should I bring to my pet's first visit?+
Please bring any previous medical records, vaccination history, current medications, and a list of questions or concerns.
Do you treat both dogs and cats?+
Yes. Our team provides comprehensive veterinary care for dogs and cats of all ages.
Do you accept pet insurance?+
We accept payment at the time of service and can provide detailed invoices for you to submit to your pet insurance provider.
How much does a veterinary visit cost?+
A standard wellness examination at Paws starts at $95. Additional costs may apply depending on vaccinations, laboratory testing, medications, or other services required.
What are your hours?+
We're open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Hours may vary on holidays.
